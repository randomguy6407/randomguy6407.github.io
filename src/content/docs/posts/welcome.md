Welcome everyone to my blog! I will be covering mainly cybersecurity related topics in here, most of which will be related to web exploitation (since that is my main category after all). I might also cover some binary exploitation, but i'm not extremely good at that so just a fair warning if i made some mistakes while making my articles on pwn.

For context, i made this blog with the help of claude, starting from an [astro template](https://astro.build/themes/details/lucode-starlight-theme/). It took around 3-4 days and many claude / gpt models helped add extra content to the template, alongside reshaping it from a documentation-style website into a blog. Initially, i took opus 4.6 and 4.7 to help me recreate the website, and later on utilized opus 4.8 & pre-banned fable 5 to polish it. After a month-ish of not working on this, i used gpt 5.6 sol to help refine this website further and post un-banned fable 5 to help smooth off remaining problems.

I hope to document some interesting stuff i discover, over here, and document the CVEs that i found. I will also publish some research in here, as well as perhaps posting some intermediate content / CTF writeups to smoothen out the website. I also hope you leave this website learning a new thing or two 🙂
